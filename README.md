**Flance4u** is a modern freelancing platform that connects freelancers with clients across various industries. It offers a secure, user-friendly environment for both freelancers and clients.

## Features

- **For Freelancers**: Showcase your skills, apply for jobs, and get paid securely.
- **For Clients**: Hire freelancers, manage projects, and make secure payments.

## Getting Started

1. **Sign Up**: Create an account as a freelancer or client.
2. **Create a Profile**: Showcase your skills (for freelancers) or post jobs (for clients).
3. **Start Working**: Freelancers can apply for projects, and clients can hire the best talent.
